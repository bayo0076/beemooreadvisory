The Gap We Close

External ambition meets local market reality

Nigeria is Africa's largest consumer market and a strategic entry point into the wider region. But success here rarely turns on the quality of your business case. It turns on local understanding, relationship navigation, timing and execution discipline.

Most foreign entrants fail on process, not on strategy — capital imported without the right certificate, a company under-capitalised below the statutory floor, a partner chosen on charm rather than diligence. All of it avoidable. That is the entire job.

Core Services

Four ways clients work with us

Tap any service to see who it is for, the problem it solves, and what you actually receive.

Who it is for
Any foreign buyer, investor or company about to contract with, pay or partner with a Nigerian entity they have not met in person.
The problem
Nigerian corporate information is fragmented and easy to dress up. A convincing website, a company registration number and a warehouse photograph prove almost nothing on their own.
What we do
  • Corporate legitimacy and CAC filing verification
  • Ownership, directorship and beneficial-interest checks
  • Operational verification — does the capacity claimed actually exist
  • Physical site and warehouse confirmation where relevant
  • Reputational and market-standing enquiry
  • Red-flag identification and commercial intelligence
What you receive
A written verification report with findings, evidence, unresolved questions and a clear recommendation — proceed, proceed with conditions, or walk away.
Practical outcome
You either contract with confidence, or you avoid a loss that would have cost many times the fee.
Request Due Diligence
Who it is for
Foreign companies committing to a Nigerian presence — subsidiary, joint venture, distribution operation or free-zone entity.
The problem
The sequence matters more than the paperwork. Import capital before your bank issues the eCCI and profit repatriation becomes a problem you cannot fully solve later.
What we do
  • Sector regulator identification and licence-capital assessment
  • CAC incorporation coordination at the correct share capital
  • NIPC registration and Ministry of Interior business permit
  • Banking introductions to institutions experienced in eCCI
  • Capital importation and eCCI guidance, tranche by tranche
  • Expatriate quota, permits and regulatory orientation
  • Local representation, stakeholder mapping and partner identification
  • Launch and first-trade support
What you receive
A sequenced entry roadmap with owners and dates, coordination with qualified counsel and bankers, and written progress reporting throughout.
Practical outcome
A properly constituted Nigerian operation with a clean capital trail — and therefore a clean route for taking profit out.

Discuss an Advisory Engagement
Who it is for
Foreign buyers seeking Nigerian products and suppliers, Nigerian suppliers seeking qualified international buyers, and companies looking for partnerships or acquisitions.
The problem
Nigerian export chains are long and intermediated. Buyers routinely deal with someone three steps from the goods, which is where both the margin and the risk quietly sit.
What we do
  • Identify Nigerian suppliers against your commercial criteria
  • Verify capability, capacity and legitimacy before introduction
  • Structure and make the introduction
  • Support negotiation and commercial terms
  • Support export execution, documentation and inspection
  • Execute the export directly where that is the agreed structure
What you receive
A ranked, verified counterparty shortlist with written notes, coordinated introductions, and execution support through to shipment.
Fee basis
A success or finder's fee on concluded transactions where appropriate, a fixed sourcing retainer, or direct execution margin — agreed in writing before work starts.
