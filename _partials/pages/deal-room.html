How We Get Paid

Fee structures, stated plainly

Agreed in writing before any work begins. No structure below is charged twice for the same piece of work.

Model 01

Success fee

A percentage of concluded transaction value on trade, partnership or acquisition mandates. We are paid when the deal closes, which aligns us with you closing a good one rather than any one.

Model 02

Fixed project fee

For defined deliverables — a due diligence report, a sector brief, a partner search, an entry roadmap. Quoted after scoping, with an agreed timeline.

Model 03

Monthly retainer

For clients with live Nigerian exposure needing continuous representation, monitoring and reporting. Fixed scope, fixed fee, written monthly.

⚑ Where a mandate involves direct export execution rather than facilitation, the commercial structure is different again and is set out in full before anything moves. We will not proceed on an unwritten fee understanding.
