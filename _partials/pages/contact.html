Before You Write

Five things people usually ask

Q

Is the first call really free?

Yes — thirty minutes, no obligation. We quote only after understanding the work, because a partner search in marine logistics and one in packaged food are not the same job.

Q

Can you handle the legal filings?

No. We are not a law firm. We map the sequence, coordinate qualified counsel and bankers, and make sure nothing happens out of order. The filings themselves go through licensed professionals.

Q

How fast is due diligence?

A basic corporate and reputational verification is typically days. Anything requiring physical site confirmation outside Lagos takes longer. We give you a timeline before you commit, not after.

Q

Do you work outside Nigeria?

Nigeria is our depth. We support wider West African work through the same network and take regional mandates where we can genuinely add value rather than just bill for it.

Q

What if Nigeria isn't right for us?

Then that is what you hear on the free call, with the specific conditions that would change the answer. It is the most common honest outcome.

Q

I am a Nigerian supplier, not a buyer

Still relevant. We work both sides — connecting export-ready Nigerian suppliers with qualified international buyers. Send your capacity, certifications and target markets.
