The Founder

Adebayo "Bayo" Olawunmi

Founder & Chief Executive, Bee Moore Advisory

Adebayo spent more than sixteen years covering Nigerian business, government, policy and the economy as a working journalist before founding Bee Moore Advisory. That background is not decoration on a consulting page — it is the firm's actual method.

Sixteen years of reporting on Nigerian institutions teaches things that cannot be researched remotely: which agency actually decides a matter and which merely announces it, how to get a straight answer out of a ministry, how regulation moves from rumour to gazette, and how to tell an operator with real capacity from one with a good deck.

"Journalism taught me to verify first, ask the difficult questions and follow the facts. Bee Moore Advisory applies that same discipline to markets, companies, partners and transactions."

Market-entry work fails for the same reason weak reporting fails — someone accepted a convenient claim without checking it. A partner's stated distribution reach. A regulator's informal assurance. A cost estimate that assumes nothing goes wrong. The job is to check, and to say so plainly when the answer is inconvenient.

What the sixteen years actually gave us

  • Working knowledge of how Nigerian institutions genuinely operate
  • Research and verification discipline applied to counterparties, not just stories
  • Regulatory and policy literacy — reading the instrument, not the coverage
  • Stakeholder access and interviewing experience across government and industry
  • A trained ability to separate market fact from market hype
  • Sixteen years of continuous observation of the same markets

Bee Moore Advisory is an independent business. Nothing here implies endorsement, sponsorship or affiliation from any employer, newsroom or government body.

Beyond the Firm

The network is the work

Alongside Bee Moore Advisory, Adebayo co-founded and serves as Executive Director of the Concerned Nation Builders Initiative, a CAC-registered NGO delivering community development projects across the five Local Government Areas of Lagos State's Epe Division. He is also Co-Founder and Country Director for Nigeria at Fovare, an edtech platform expanding access to education across emerging markets.

These are not a list of titles. Government relations work is easier when you have actually delivered projects alongside a state ministry. Cross-border partnership work is easier when you already run one.
