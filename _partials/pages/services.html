Core Services

Four ways clients work with us

01 · Due Diligence

Partner & Company Due Diligence

Nigerian corporate information is fragmented and easy to dress up. A convincing website, a registration number and a warehouse photograph prove almost nothing on their own. We verify corporate legitimacy and CAC filings, ownership and beneficial interest, whether claimed operational capacity actually exists, physical sites where relevant, and market reputation — then set out the red flags plainly.

You receive: a written verification report with findings, evidence, unresolved questions and a clear recommendation — proceed, proceed with conditions, or walk away.

Request Due Diligence
02 · Market Entry

Market Entry — Done For You

The sequence matters more than the paperwork. We identify your sector regulator and its capital floor, coordinate CAC incorporation at the correct share capital, handle NIPC registration and the Ministry of Interior business permit, introduce banks that handle eCCI competently, guide capital importation tranche by tranche, and support expatriate quota, permits, stakeholder mapping and launch.

You receive: a sequenced roadmap with owners and dates, coordination with qualified counsel and bankers, and written progress reporting throughout.

Plan My Nigeria Entry
03 · Advisory

Strategic Advisory & Retainers

Nigerian regulation, FX policy and sector conditions move faster than most foreign boards review them. We run FDI strategy and entry-route modelling, bespoke research and sector briefs, competitor and incumbent mapping, regulatory change monitoring, stakeholder and decision-maker mapping, and continuous opportunity monitoring.

You receive: scheduled written briefings, direct founder access, and someone in Lagos whose job is to notice when something moves.

Discuss an Advisory Engagement
04 · Deals & Trade

Deal & Partner Facilitation, Export Execution

Nigerian export chains are long and heavily intermediated — buyers routinely deal with someone three steps from the goods, which is where both the margin and the risk quietly sit. We identify suppliers against your criteria, verify capability and capacity before any introduction, structure the introduction, support negotiation and export execution, and where agreed execute the export directly.

You receive: a ranked, verified counterparty shortlist with written notes, coordinated introductions, and execution support through to shipment.

Boundaries

What we do not do

Stated up front, because the firms that leave this vague are the ones you should worry about.

We are not lawyers or accountants

We do not file, advise on tax positions, or give legal opinions. We map the sequence, coordinate qualified professionals, and make sure nothing happens out of order.

We do not guarantee outcomes

No guaranteed returns, no guaranteed contract awards, no guaranteed market-entry success. Anyone offering those in this market is selling you something else.

We do not trade influence

We do not offer, imply or facilitate influence over public-sector decisions, and we will end an engagement that moves in that direction.

We do not take both sides quietly

We take no commission from the partners, suppliers or banks we assess for you. Any conflict is disclosed before work begins, or we decline.
